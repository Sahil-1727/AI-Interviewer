BEFORE YOU BEGIN

Before Your Interview Begins

Please review these guidelines to ensure a smooth and professional AI interview experience.

1. Interview Environment

  • Sit in a quiet, well-lit room with minimal background noise.
  • Preferably use headphones or earphones with a microphone.
  • Avoid loud fans, television, music, and nearby conversations.
  • Ensure you have a stable internet connection.
  • Check your microphone and camera before beginning. The camera is optional unless explicitly required.

2. Answering Questions

  • Listen carefully to each question before answering.
  • Speak clearly and at a natural pace.
    • {% if livekit_primary %}
  • Speak naturally; the LiveKit interviewer listens for the end of your turn and supports interruptions.
  • You can say "I'm done" or click Done Speaking when you want to finish your answer.
    • {% else %}
  • Begin speaking within {{ voice_config.INITIAL_ANSWER_TIMEOUT_SECONDS|int }} seconds, or the question will be marked unanswered.
    • {% if voice_config.STT_PROVIDER == 'deepgram' %}
  • Once you start answering, about 2–3 seconds of silence may automatically finalize your answer.
  • If you resume speaking before finalization, the silence timer resets.
    • {% else %}
  • Once you start answering, 5 consecutive seconds of silence will automatically finalize and submit your answer.
  • If you resume speaking before those 5 seconds end, the silence timer resets.
    • {% endif %}
  • You can also say "I'm done" or click Done Speaking to submit immediately.
    • {% endif %}
  • Say "Repeat the question" if you want the AI interviewer to repeat the current question.

3. Interview Controls

  • The interview progresses automatically after each completed answer.
  • The AI may ask an adaptive follow-up question or proceed to the next question.
  • You can pause and resume the interview when necessary, including when you need more time to think.
  • You can click End Interview at any time.
  • Ending early will generate a provisional scorecard based on your completed, evaluated answers.
  • Unanswered questions will not automatically receive zero scores.

4. Privacy

  • Your microphone is used to capture and transcribe spoken answers.
  • Your camera currently provides a live preview only; video is not recorded or analyzed.
